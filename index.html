Welcome to my Towers of Hanoi game

Instructions:

  • The game uses three pegs standing upright and a set of disks with holes in the center. The disks are all different sizes, At the start of the game, all the disks are stacked on the first peg, arranged from largest on the bottom to smallest on top, forming a tower shape.

  • Goal: Move the entire tower of disks from the first peg onto any other peg, keeping them in the same order(largest on bottom, smallest on top).

  • The rules:

    1. You can only move the top disk on any peg, you cannot move a disk underneath another disk.
    2. A larger disk can never go on top of a smaller disk, it must either be moved onto an empty peg, or a disk that's larger.
    3. You can place a disk on any of the three pegs, as long as you follow rule #2.
